Goblin Health Local Sync

A personal-use tool for keeping your own health data on your own disk.

This is a single-user tool. It reads your Google Health (Fitbit) data through the Google Health API and stores it in a local SQLite database on your own machine. It is not a service, there is no sign-up, and nothing is collected from anyone.

  • What it does: calls the Google Health API as you, for your account only.
  • Where the data goes: a SQLite file on your own disk. Nothing is uploaded or shared.
  • Who can use it: the owner of the Google Cloud project it runs under, and no one else. The OAuth client is unverified and Google caps it at 100 users; exactly one account is authorised.

Built on google-health-local-sync (MIT). This fork is maintained at github.com/cflux/goblin-health-local-sync.
